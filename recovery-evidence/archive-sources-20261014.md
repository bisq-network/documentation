# Archived wiki source records

The [WikiTeam archive labelled 14 October 2026](https://archive.org/details/wiki-bisq.wiki-20261014) contains the 63 redirect records and 18 template definitions preserved under `public/wiki-source/archive-redirects/` and `public/wiki-source/archive-templates/`. The accompanying manifests record the latest available revision ID, revision time, source URL and SHA-256 for each file. Fourteen template definitions correspond to placeholders identified in the recovered articles. Two redirect records also contain substantive trailing text; their complete source files are retained.

`npm test` verifies the record counts, source paths, revision metadata, original content hashes and directory inventories. The existing build separately verifies the 209 original article bodies and their source files. These archived records are historical source evidence; their presence alone does not establish that every revision was captured or that the instructions are current.
