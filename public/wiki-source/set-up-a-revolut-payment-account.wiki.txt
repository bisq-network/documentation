First download, install Bisq and create a backup (copy seed words - see [[How to back up your Bisq wallet seed]]).

Now you can set up a [[fiat]] payment account, that you will use to pay or receive payments when trading on Bisq.

1. Go to '''Account''' tab on Bisq

2. Click on '''Add new Account'''

3. Select '''Revolut''' as your payment method

4. Input your Revolut user name. Select your currencies.

5. Click on '''Save New Account'''.

You can now go to '''Market''' tab and start buying and selling. See [[How to buy BTC on Bisq with EUR]].
