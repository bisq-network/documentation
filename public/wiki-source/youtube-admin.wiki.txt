The '''YouTube Admin''' maintains the Bisq YouTube channel, uploading videos and answering comments as necessary.

== Duties ==
* Upload videos as necessary
* Answer comments from YouTube users
* Feature talks, reviews, and other videos on playlists and the channel's front page for easier discovery

== Rights ==
* "Manager" rights over the [https://youtube.com/c/bisq-network YouTube channel]
