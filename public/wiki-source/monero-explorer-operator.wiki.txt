== Team ==

== Duties ==
* Run an instance of the [https://github.com/moneroexamples/onion-monero-blockchain-explorer Onion Monero Blockchain Explorer]
* Make your Monero Explorer instance available over HTTPS clearnet and Tor V3 HS onion, enable HSTS and DNSSEC
* Update the [https://github.com/bisq-network/bisq/blob/master/core/src/main/java/bisq/core/user/Preferences.java#L130 Bisq code] to contain the FQDN clearnet hostname and Tor V3 HS onion of your Monero Explorer

== Rights ==
* Claim $100/month compensation

== Bonding ==
* Post 20,000 BSQ bond for Explorer Operator
