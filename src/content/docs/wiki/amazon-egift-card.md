See more details on <a href="https://www.amazon.com/Amazon-1_US_Email-eGift-Card/dp/B004LLIKVU" rel="noopener noreferrer">Amazon's product page</a>.</p>&#10;</aside>&#10;<p>You can fill in an email address or a phone number:</p>&#10;<ol>&#10;<li>Which one you pick (email address or phone number) only affects how the bitcoin seller will receive the eGift Card (i.e., via email or text message)</li>&#10;<li>The phone number or email address provided here does not need to be linked to your Amazon account. Once the bitcoin seller has received the eGift Card, they can add it to any Amazon account.</li>&#10;</ol>&#10;<figure class="wiki-source-image" id="source-image-1"><a href="../../media/wiki/fd78831e0e63f779e81e88b2f792d3c4cfce44d92358cbecd78e19fa3d16bc6b.png"><img alt="Creating an Amazon eGift card payment account in Bisq." decoding="async" height="522" loading="lazy" src="../../media/wiki/fd78831e0e63f779e81e88b2f792d3c4cfce44d92358cbecd78e19fa3d16bc6b.png" width="1906"></a><figcaption>Creating an Amazon eGift card payment account in Bisq.<br><small>Historical full-size recovered source. <a href="../../recovered-media/">Provenance catalogue</a>.</small></figcaption></figure>&#10;<p><br>&#10;</p>&#10;<h2 id="buying-bitcoin-with-an-amazon-egift-card"> Buying bitcoin with an Amazon eGift Card </h2>&#10;<h3 id="step-1-log-in-to-amazon"> Step 1. Log in to Amazon </h3>&#10;<p>First, log in to the Amazon website you'll be buying the eGift card on. <strong>If trading a currency used on multiple Amazon sites (e.g. EUR currency has amazon.de, amazon.it, amazon.es, etc), please confirm with your peer (through trader chat) which site should be used to purchase the gift card.</strong> You should be able to use your existing Amazon account to log in to any Amazon site (e.g. you can sign in to amazon.de using an account created on amazon.com).</p>&#10;<h3 id="step-2-customize-egift-card"> Step 2. Customize eGift card </h3>&#10;<p>It's recommended to customize the eGift Card to reduce the chances the gift card is seen as suspicious. Feel free to customize the card design, message, etc. Just remember not to include anything about Bisq or bitcoin anywhere.</p>&#10;<ul>&#10;<li><strong>Amount</strong>: Choose the amount of fiat to send that matches your trade amount. It is recommended to do multiple gift cards of 100 USD or less, as Amazon can consider gift cards of higher amounts to be fraudulent.</li>&#10;<li><strong>Delivery</strong>: Email address or phone number of the seller</li>&#10;<li><strong>To</strong>: eGift recipient information as prompted in the Bisq trade.</li>&#10;<li><strong>Message</strong>: This should be left blank. If you would like to use anything other than a blank message make sure to use trader chat to confirm it with your trading peer. If not a message has been confirmed please leave blank.'''</li>&#10;</ul>&#10;<figure class="wiki-source-image" id="source-image-2"><a href="../../media/wiki/fbd6ff92660396a436d835b364a58dfea7914b2256e0d994daa700634358c846.png"><img alt="Customize your gift card." decoding="async" height="809" loading="lazy" src="../../media/wiki/fbd6ff92660396a436d835b364a58dfea7914b2256e0d994daa700634358c846.png" width="1600"></a><figcaption>Customize your gift card.<br><small>Historical thumbnail; original resolution not recovered. <a href="../../recovered-media/">Provenance catalogue</a>.</small></figcaption></figure>&#10;<p><br>&#10;</p>&#10;<h3 id="step-3-make-payment"> Step 3. Make Payment  </h3>&#10;<p>After your card is ready go, you just have to pay for it! Amazon accepts debit and credit cards, but you cannot pay for an eGift card with another eGift card (or any other Amazon credit balance).</p>&#10;<p>Note: your payment details will NOT be shared with the Seller. The only information they will see is the information you entered to customize the gift card in step 2.</p>&#10;<p>After paying, you'll receive an email receipt from Amazon—make sure you keep this email in case of any issues!</p>&#10;<p>Then make sure you mark your payment as sent in Bisq.</p>&#10;<h3 id="step-4-seller-gets-notification"> Step 4. Seller Gets Notification  </h3>&#10;<p>Once the buyer sends the gift card, the seller will receive an email or SMS message letting them know about their gift card.</p>&#10;<aside class="wiki-admonition" role="note"><p><strong>Note</strong></p><p>If the seller didn't receive a notification, you can have Amazon send another notification (email or SMS) from the Recent Orders section on Amazon.</p>&#10;</aside>&#10;<p>Upon checking the payment (making sure it's the right amount etc), the seller must confirm the payment in Bisq to complete the trade.</p>&#10;<p>Optionally, the seller can also acknowledge the gift card through Amazon, which would send the buyer an email letting them know the seller has received their gift card.</p>&#10;<h2 id="see-also"> See also </h2>&#10;<ul>&#10;<li><a href="../payment-methods/">Payment methods</a></li>&#10;</ul>&#10;</div>
