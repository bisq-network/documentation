The **Fee Reimbursement Agent** refunds traders who have lost fees on trades through software bugs.

For more information see: [Failed Trades - Reimbursement of Trade Fees and Miner Fees](../failed-trades-reimbursement-of-trade-fees-and-miner-fees/)

Reimbursements are made with BTC. Traders seeking fee reimbursements must provide a screenshot and details of trades for which they wish fee reimbursements (more details are in the [issue template](https://github.com/bisq-network/support/issues/new?assignees=&labels=&template=reimbursement-template.md&title=Fee+reimbursement+for+trade+XXXXX)).

## Duties {#duties}

- Examine every Github proposal, verify that the taker and maker fee address are mentioned in the issue. then move to \"ready\" [Lost fee reimbursements](https://github.com/bisq-network/support/projects/1)
- Ensure reimbursement request was submitted within 3 months of the trade completing as per the accepted proposal [here](https://github.com/bisq-network/proposals/issues/369)
- When starting to process an individual issue, move it to \"In progress\". Then open the terminal.
- Make a clone of the support repository.
- Enter ./helpers/automate.sh
- Add issue number, verify the information is correct.
- Follow the next steps and publish. move issue to \"staged\"
- Now make the reimbursement and close issue.
