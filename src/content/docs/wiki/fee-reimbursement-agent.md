<div class="wiki-recovered-article" lang="en"><p>The <strong>Fee Reimbursement Agent</strong> refunds traders who have lost fees on trades through software bugs.</p>&#10;<p>For more information see: <a href="../failed-trades-reimbursement-of-trade-fees-and-miner-fees/">Failed Trades - Reimbursement of Trade Fees and Miner Fees</a></p>&#10;<p>Reimbursements are made with BTC.
