<div class="wiki-recovered-article" lang="en"><p>The <strong>Payment Method Maintainer</strong> vets new payment methods for use in Bisq, maintains existing ones, and monitors performance.</p>
<p>This is not a technical maintainer role—meaning it is not about development, but rather about managing the process for adding and maintaining payment methods.</p>

<span class="wiki-legacy-anchor" id="Duties" aria-hidden="true"></span><h2 id="duties"> Duties </h2>
<ul>
<li>Tasks
<ul>
<li>Monthly reporting on payment method volume and health</li>
<li>Monthly reporting on role issues</li>
<li>Payment methods prioritization</li>
<li>Payment methods requirements verification and required fields description</li>
<li>Maintain payment methods roadmap</li>
<li>Develop payment method addition process</li>
</ul></li>
<li>Goals
<ul>
<li>Proactively find payment methods to support Bisq's growth in coordination with growth experiments</li>
<li>Support to proposed payment methods addition</li>
<li>Develop payment methods maintainer role</li>
</ul></li>
<li>KPIs
<ul>
<li>% of transaction problems related to payment methods</li>
<li>% of market share represented by supported payment methods (TBD)</li>
<li>% arbitration requests / payment method, speed, etc.</li>
</ul></li>
</ul>
