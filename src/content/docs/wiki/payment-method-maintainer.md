<div class="wiki-recovered-article" lang="en"><p>The <strong>Payment Method Maintainer</strong> vets new payment methods for use in Bisq, maintains existing ones, and monitors performance.</p>&#10;<p>This is not a technical maintainer role—meaning it is not about development, but rather about managing the process for adding and maintaining payment methods.</p>&#10;<h2 id="issue"> Issue </h2>&#10;<a href="https://github.com/bisq-network/roles/issues/38" rel="noopener noreferrer">https://github.com/bisq-network/roles/issues/38</a>&#10;<h2 id="team"> Team </h2>&#10;<a href="https://github.com/orgs/bisq-network/teams/payment-method-maintainers" rel="noopener noreferrer">@bisq-network/payment-method-maintainers</a>&#10;<h2 id="duties"> Duties </h2>&#10;<ul>&#10;<li>Tasks&#10;<ul>&#10;<li>Monthly reporting on payment method volume and health</li>&#10;<li>Monthly reporting on role issues</li>&#10;<li>Payment methods prioritization</li>&#10;<li>Payment methods requirements verification and required fields description</li>&#10;<li>Maintain payment methods roadmap</li>&#10;<li>Develop payment method addition process</li>&#10;</ul></li>&#10;<li>Goals&#10;<ul>&#10;<li>Proactively find payment methods to support Bisq's growth in coordination with growth experiments</li>&#10;<li>Support to proposed payment methods addition</li>&#10;<li>Develop payment methods maintainer role</li>&#10;</ul></li>&#10;<li>KPIs&#10;<ul>&#10;<li>% of transaction problems related to payment methods</li>&#10;<li>% of market share represented by supported payment methods (TBD)</li>&#10;<li>% arbitration requests / payment method, speed, etc.</li>&#10;</ul></li>&#10;</ul>&#10;<h2 id="rights"> Rights </h2>&#10;<p>None.</p>&#10;<h2 id="bonding"> Bonding </h2>&#10;<p>Not required.</p>&#10;</div>
