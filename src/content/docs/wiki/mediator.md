<div class="wiki-recovered-article" lang="en"><p><strong>Mediators</strong> help to resolve disputes between two trading parties if they cannot settle a dispute on their own. A mediator can only <em>propose</em> a payout to settle a dispute—the payout can only take place if both parties agree and sign the transaction to make it happen. Current mediators are:</p>
