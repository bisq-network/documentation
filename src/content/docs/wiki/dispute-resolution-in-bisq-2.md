Sometimes, Bisq trades don\'t go smoothly, and **dispute resolution** is required to determine an outcome. Dispute resolution in [Bisq 2](../bisq-2/) varies depending on the [trade protocol](../trade-protocols/) that was used.

Most issues on Bisq are minor and easily resolved when traders communicate with each other. Mediation is intended to resolve the vast majority of remaining issues.

## Trade Protocols {#trade-protocols}

[Bisq 2](../bisq-2/) will offer users the choice of multiple [trade protocols](../trade-protocols/). A [trade protocol](../trade-protocols/) serves as the fundamental framework for trading Bitcoin in a secure way. Each trade protocol presents its own set of advantages and trade-offs, including:

- Security mechanisms
- Dispute mechanisms
- Security deposit requirements

[Bisq 2](../bisq-2/) offers users the flexibility to select the optimal protocol aligning with their requirements and preferences. All [trade protocols](../trade-protocols/) will be accessible from the [Bisq 2](../bisq-2/) application independently. Users will be able to switch between [trade protocols](../trade-protocols/) for different trades should they wish.

### Bisq Easy {#bisq-easy}

[Bisq Easy](../bisq-easy/) is a [trade protocol](../trade-protocols/) for [Bisq 2](../bisq-2/), designed to facilitate the acquisition of bitcoin with zero trade fees and without the requirement of a [security deposit](../security-deposit/). [Bisq Easy](../bisq-easy/) is a social [trade protocol](../trade-protocols/) intended for novice bitcoin buyers seeking a non-KYC option and aiming to be efficiently onboarded by experienced and knowledgeable sellers that are familiar with the Bisq platform.

Security mechanisms: [Reputation](../reputation/) Dispute mechanisms: [Trade Chat](../dispute-resolution-in-bisq-2/#trade-chat) and [Mediation (for Bisq Easy)](../dispute-resolution-in-bisq-2/#mediation-in-bisq-easy)

## Dispute Options {#dispute-options}

</span>

### Trade Chat {#trade-chat}

If you\'re having any issues completing a trade, or if you just want to keep your counterparty informed (e.g., in case you\'re delayed completing your end of the deal), you can send your counterparty a message through Bisq\'s end-to-end encrypted chat mechanism. This allows traders to communicate with each other throughout the course of a trade to resolve potential trade issues quickly without involving a third party like a [mediator](../mediator/).

<span class="wiki-legacy-anchor" id="Mediation_in_Bisq_Easy" aria-hidden="true">
</span>

### Mediation in Bisq Easy {#mediation-in-bisq-easy}

Mediation is available within Bisq Easy at any time. You can initiate it by selecting the \'request mediation\' link on the trade screen. A [mediator](../mediator/) will then join the trade chat and gather information to assist as much as possible. Since Bisq Easy trades do not require a security deposit, the mediator\'s abilities are somewhat limited. However, they can still provide valuable assistance and report serious rule violations to moderators. They cannot enforce an outcome or guarantee recovery.

## If all else fails{#if-all-else-fails}

One advantage of a flat, decentralized governance structure that encourages user involvement is that no user should ever feel hindered from receiving a fair trade outcome because of a single individual\'s decision. Bisq isn\'t a black-box corporation with inscrutable processes. It\'s an open software project that is governed openly.

Therefore, for whatever reason, if the process above doesn\'t work for you, you can submit a [reimbursement request](../making-a-reimbursement-request/) yourself in the [Bisq DAO](../dao-user-reference/#bisq-dao). **The [Bisq DAO](../dao-user-reference/#bisq-dao), not any single individual, is the ultimate arbiter of policy and decision-making for all things Bisq.** A request is not a guaranteed reimbursement or a Bisq 1 escrow/arbitration payout for a Bisq Easy trade.
