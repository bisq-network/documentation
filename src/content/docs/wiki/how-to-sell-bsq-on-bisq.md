First download, install Bisq and create a backup (copy seed words - see [How to back up your Bisq wallet seed](../how-to-back-up-your-bisq-wallet-seed/)).

Now you can trade. To sell BSQ follow these simple steps:

1\. On **Market** tab select BSQ/BTC

2\. Click on **SELL BSQ**

3\. Select an offer and click on **TAKE OFFER**

4\. Review offer details including amount, price. Then click on **TAKE OFFER TO BUY BTC**.

5\. Click on **CONFIRM TAKE OFFER**.

6\. BSQ/BTC swap is now complete. Wait for blockchain confirmation.

7\. Check that you have received BSQ in your wallet.

BSQ/BTC swaps are **Atomic Swaps**. This means that they are either completed or not. They cannot be stuck or incomplete. They have no counterparty risk and require no **trusted third party** to be involved.

- For more short guides see [Howto](../howto/). You can also find support on **[Matrix](https://bisq.chat)**.
