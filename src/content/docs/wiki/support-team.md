The Support Team is responsible for providing excellent [support](../user-support/) for Bisq users. This includes:

- Handling individual support cases, including [mediation](../mediation/) and <span class="wiki-link-gap">refunds <small>[unrecovered article: refunds]</small></span>
- Maintaining the Bisq <span class="wiki-link-gap">docs website <small>[unrecovered article: Docs]</small></span>
- Maintaining support-related articles here on the Bisq <span class="wiki-link-gap">wiki <small>[unrecovered article: wiki]</small></span>.
