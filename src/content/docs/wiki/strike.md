</h2>&#10;<p>It is easy to add your Strike account to Bisq.</p>&#10;<ol>&#10;<li>Go to the top menu in Bisq and select Account.</li>&#10;<li>Then select National Currency Accounts.</li>&#10;<li>Click add new account</li>&#10;<li>Select Strike</li>&#10;<li>Complete the details</li>&#10;<li>Choose to use a custom account name this helps makes it easier for you to manage multiple payment methods in Bisq (this will be visible only to you, for example: Strike - USD - Jane Doe)</li>&#10;<li>Click save new account</li>&#10;</ol>&#10;<p>Make sure all the details are correct when you add.</p>&#10;<aside class="wiki-admonition" role="note"><p><strong>Warning</strong></p><p>Please make sure to include your Strike username.</p>&#10;<p>In Bisq, Strike is used for fiat to fiat payments only.</p>&#10;<p>Please make sure you are aware of the Strike limits:</p>&#10;<p>Users who have registered with only their email, name, and phone number have the following limits:</p>&#10;<ul>&#10;<li>$100 maximum per deposit</li>&#10;<li>$1,000 maximum total deposits per week</li>&#10;<li>$100 maximum per payment</li>&#10;</ul>&#10;<p>Users can increase their limits by providing Strike more information have the following limits:</p>&#10;<ul>&#10;<li>$1,000 maximum per deposit</li>&#10;<li>$1,000 maximum total deposits per week</li>&#10;<li>$1,000 maximum per payment</li>&#10;</ul>&#10;<p>If you trade over the above limits your trade might be cancelled and there could be a penalty.</p>&#10;</aside>&#10;<span class="wiki-legacy-anchor" id="How_do_I_buy_BTC_with_my_Strike_account?" aria-hidden="true"></span><h2 id="how-do-i-buy-btc-with-my-strike-account"> How do I buy BTC with my Strike account? </h2>&#10;<p>You can buy BTC with Strike on Bisq in 2 waysː</p>&#10;<ol>&#10;<li>Make an offer to buy BTC on the USD market.</li>&#10;<li>Take an offer to buy BTC on the USD market.</li>&#10;</ol>&#10;<p>When the offer is confirmed Bisq will provide you with the BTC Sellers Strike details for you to complete the payment. You should send payment immediately and confirm you have done so in Bisq by clicking 'payment started'.</p>&#10;<p>If you have the option to include a payment reference please leave it blank. If your bank requires one, please just use your name that matches your bank account.</p>&#10;<p>Once the BTC Seller receives your payment they will confirm in Bisq and your security deposit and your purchased trade amount will be available in your wallet.</p>&#10;<aside class="wiki-admonition" role="note"><p><strong>Warning</strong></p><p>Please send payment only to the BTC Seller's Strike username as provided in Bisq.</p>&#10;<p>The maximum trade size is $1,000 maximum per payment.</p>&#10;<p>If you trade over the above limits your trade might be cancelled and there could be a penalty.</p>&#10;</aside>&#10;<span class="wiki-legacy-anchor" id="How_do_I_sell_BTC_with_my_Strike_account?" aria-hidden="true"></span><h2 id="how-do-i-sell-btc-with-my-strike-account"> How do I sell BTC with my Strike account? </h2>&#10;<p>You can sell BTC with Strike on Bisq in 2 waysː</p>&#10;<ol>&#10;<li>Make an offer to sell BTC on the USD market.</li>&#10;<li>Take an offer to sell BTC on the USD market.</li>&#10;</ol>&#10;<p>When the offer is confirmed Bisq will provide with the BTC Buyer with your Strike details for you to complete the payment.
