<div class="wiki-recovered-article" lang="en"><p>Sometimes Bisq trades don't go smoothly, and <strong>dispute resolution</strong> is required to determine an outcome.</p>
<p>Dispute resolution on Bisq has three formal levels: trader chat, <a href="../mediation/">medition</a>, and <a href="../arbitration/">arbitration</a>.</p>
<p>Most issues on Bisq are minor and easily resolved when traders communicate with each other. <a href="../mediation/">Mediation</a> is intended to resolve the vast majority of remaining issues. <a href="../arbitration/">Arbitration</a> is a rare last resort measure for extreme scenarios.</p>
<span class="wiki-legacy-anchor" id="Level_1:_Trader_chat" aria-hidden="true"></span><h2 id="level-1-trader-chat"> Level 1: Trader chat </h2>
<p>If you're having any issues completing a trade, or if you just want to keep your counterparty informed (e.g., in case you're delayed completing your end of the deal), you can send your counterparty a message through Bisq's end-to-end encrypted chat mechanism. This allows traders to communicate with each other throughout the course of a trade to resolve potential trade issues quickly without involving a third party like a <a href="../mediator/">mediator</a> or <a href="../arbitrator/">arbitrator</a>.</p>

<span class="wiki-legacy-anchor" id="Trader_chat_rules" aria-hidden="true"></span><h3 id="trader-chat-rules"> Trader chat rules </h3>
<p>Hopefully, by following these rules you can work directly with your trading peer to solve issues quickly and amicably.</p>
<span class="wiki-legacy-anchor" id="Good_Communication_is_Key" aria-hidden="true"></span><h4 id="good-communication-is-key"> Good Communication is Key </h4>
<p>Good communication between traders can resolve most disputes. It's recommended for traders to communicate any issues they're having to keep their peer informed.</p>
<p>However, <strong>responding to chat messages is always optional</strong>. If you’re not comfortable responding, don’t. You have no obligation to communicate over this medium with traders. You must, however, respond to <a href="../mediator/">mediators</a> and <a href="../arbitrator/">arbitrators</a> when needed.</p>
<span class="wiki-legacy-anchor" id="Respect_a_Peer’s_Decision_to_Avoid_Responding" aria-hidden="true"></span><h4 id="respect-a-peers-decision-to-avoid-responding"> Respect a Peer’s Decision to Avoid Responding </h4>
<p>If a peer seems unresponsive or has signaled their intention to refrain from chatting, please respect it and don’t pressure them.</p>
<span class="wiki-legacy-anchor" id="Do_Not_Send_Links_of_Any_Kind" aria-hidden="true"></span><h4 id="do-not-send-links-of-any-kind"> Do Not Send Links of Any Kind </h4>
<p>For safety reasons, sending links is prohibited. If you want to share a link, describe it instead.</p>
<p><em>Not allowed:</em></p>
<p><code><code class="wiki-source-code" id="source-code-1">https://blockstream.info/tx/4b5417ec5ab6112bedf539c3b4f5a806ed539542d8b717e1c4470aa3180edce5</code></code></p>
<p><em>Allowed:</em></p>
<p><code>Hey, could you look up txid 4b5417ec5ab6112bedf539c3b4f5a806ed539542d8b717e1c4470aa3180edce5 in your favorite block explorer?</code></p>
<span class="wiki-legacy-anchor" id="Do_Not_Encourage_Trading_Away_from_Bisq" aria-hidden="true"></span><h4 id="do-not-encourage-trading-away-from-bisq"> Do Not Encourage Trading Away from Bisq </h4>
<p>Trades away from Bisq lack Bisq’s security mechanisms. If something goes wrong with an off-Bisq trade, you’re on your own.</p>
<span class="wiki-legacy-anchor" id="Do_Not_Share_Sensitive_Information" aria-hidden="true"></span><h4 id="do-not-share-sensitive-information"> Do Not Share Sensitive Information </h4>
<p>Such information is <strong>never</strong> wise to share in general and is never needed to solve trade disputes on Bisq. This includes passwords, seed words and private keys.</p>
<span class="wiki-legacy-anchor" id="Do_Not_Engage_in_Foul_Play" aria-hidden="true"></span><h4 id="do-not-engage-in-foul-play"> Do Not Engage in Foul Play </h4>
<p>Any attempts to exploit counterparties with social engineering scams, etc., are prohibited.</p>
<span class="wiki-legacy-anchor" id="Keep_Conversation_Scope_Limited_to_the_Trade" aria-hidden="true"></span><h4 id="keep-conversation-scope-limited-to-the-trade"> Keep Conversation Scope Limited to the Trade </h4>
<p>Please don’t use Bisq’s trader chat as a general-purpose messenger. Unnecessary exchanges add unnecessary strain to Bisq’s peer-to-peer network.</p>
<span class="wiki-legacy-anchor" id="Keep_Conversation_Friendly_and_Respectful" aria-hidden="true"></span><h4 id="keep-conversation-friendly-and-respectful"> Keep Conversation Friendly and Respectful </h4>
<p>Because friendlier traders resolve disputes quicker, and no one wants to deal with a jerk.</p>
<span class="wiki-legacy-anchor" id="Level_2:_Mediation" aria-hidden="true"></span><h2 id="level-2-mediation"> Level 2: Mediation </h2>
<p>If issues persist after discussing them with the counterparty, you may need to involve a <a href="../mediator/">mediator</a>.</p>
<p><strong><a href="../mediation/">Mediation</a> can be initiated at any point during or after the trade period. To do this, select the trade from the 'Open Trades' list and press 'Ctrl' + 'O'.</strong></p>
<p>In Bisq 1 v1.10.11, Alt+O also opens this prompt (Option+O on macOS); Ctrl+O and the platform shortcut are accepted too.</p>

<span class="wiki-legacy-anchor" id="How_Mediation_Works" aria-hidden="true"></span><h3 id="how-mediation-works"> How Mediation Works </h3>
<p><a href="../mediator/">Mediators</a> assess a trade situation between two users and propose a payout suggestion. They don't hold a key in the multisig escrow, so their proposal isn't authoritative.</p>
<span class="wiki-legacy-anchor" id="Trading_Peer_Expectations" aria-hidden="true"></span><h4 id="trading-peer-expectations"> Trading Peer Expectations </h4>
<ul>
<li>Cooperate with the <a href="../mediator/">mediator</a>, providing requested information (quality proof will be asked for payments or bank issues)</li>
<li>Respond to messages within 48 hours. Non-response might result in <a href="../table-of-penalties/">penalties</a>.</li>
</ul>
<span class="wiki-legacy-anchor" id="Mediator_Response_Time" aria-hidden="true"></span><h4 id="mediator-response-time"> Mediator Response Time </h4>
<p><a href="../mediator/">Mediators</a> may take up to 48 hours to respond. <strong>Please be patient</strong>.</p>
<span class="wiki-legacy-anchor" id="Payout_Suggestion" aria-hidden="true"></span><h4 id="payout-suggestion"> Payout Suggestion </h4>
<ul>
<li>If both peers agree, the payout is completed, and the trade is closed.</li>
<li>If one or both disagree, a peer can engage an <a href="../arbitrator/">arbitrator</a> to reassess the situation (see <a href="../dispute-resolution-in-bisq-1/#level-3-arbitration">arbitration</a> below).</li>
</ul>
<span class="wiki-legacy-anchor" id="When_are_you_eligible_for_a_penalty?" aria-hidden="true"></span><h3 id="when-are-you-eligible-for-a-penalty"> When are you eligible for a penalty? </h3>
<p>If a trader does not follow the <a href="../trading-rules/">trading rules</a>, they risk being <a href="../table-of-penalties/">penalized</a>. Any <a href="../table-of-penalties/">penalties</a> will be taken from the trader's BTC <a href="../security-deposit/">security deposit</a>.</p>
<p>The trader at fault will be charged the <a href="../table-of-penalties/">penalty</a>, and their <a href="../security-deposit/">security deposit</a> will be deducted a BTC amount.</p>
<p>The trader that is wronged will receive the <a href="../table-of-penalties/">BTC penalty</a> as compensation. The payout transaction for the trade will include funds from the other trader's <a href="../security-deposit/">security deposit</a>.</p>
<p>For more information about what to expect, you can see the <a href="../table-of-penalties/">table of penalties</a>. This table acts as a guideline for traders, <a href="../mediator/">mediators</a>, and <a href="../arbitrator/">arbitrators</a> to ensure <a href="../table-of-penalties/">penalty</a> are applied in a fair and impartial way.</p>
<span class="wiki-legacy-anchor" id="Who_are_mediators?" aria-hidden="true"></span><h3 id="who-are-mediators"> Who are mediators? </h3>
<p><a href="../mediator/">Mediator</a> roles are <a href="https://docs.bisq.network/user-dao-intro.html#ensure-honesty-in-high-trust-roles" rel="noopener noreferrer">bonded roles</a> in the <a href="../dao-user-reference/#bisq-dao">Bisq DAO</a>. Anyone can propose to become a <a href="../mediator/">mediator</a>, but approval will depend on the candidate's familiarity with Bisq <a href="../trading-rules/">trading rules</a> and the network’s need for more <a href="../mediator/">mediators</a> at the time a proposal is made.</p>
<p>Once a proposal to become a <a href="../mediator/">mediator</a> is approved by <a href="../dao-user-reference/#voting-cycle">DAO voting</a>, the <a href="../mediator/">mediator</a> must lock a 10,000 BSQ bond to become active. This bond helps to ensure their continued availability and performance in the role, and can be confiscated by <a href="../dao-user-reference/#voting-cycle">DAO voting</a> in extreme circumstances (negligence, foul play, prolonged absence, etc).</p>
<p>You can see more details (current role owners, regular updates, etc) on the <a href="https://github.com/bisq-network/roles/issues/83" rel="noopener noreferrer">mediator role issue</a>.</p>
<span class="wiki-legacy-anchor" id="Where_to_communicate_with_mediators?" aria-hidden="true"></span><h3 id="where-to-communicate-with-mediators"> Where to communicate with mediators? </h3>
<p>You can communicate with your <a href="../mediator/">mediator</a> directly in the Bisq app. When you initiate a trade and open a <a href="../mediation/">mediation</a>, a support ticket is automatically created within the app. You can find this in the 'Support' &gt; 'Mediation' section.</p>
<p>Communicating with <a href="../mediator/">mediators</a> inside the app is preferred.</p>
<p>In case you're unable to communicate within the Bisq app for any reason, you can also reach out to them on <a href="../matrix-bisq-chat/">Matrix</a>.</p>
<p>Find more information on <a href="../finding-your-mediator/">finding your mediator here</a>.</p>
<span class="wiki-legacy-anchor" id="How_to_find_your_mediators?" aria-hidden="true"></span><h3 id="how-to-find-your-mediators"> How to find your mediators? </h3>
<p>Your <a href="../mediator/">mediator's</a> name should be visible when you open a mediation ticket. Look for it under the 'support agent' column.</p>
<p>If, however, you have a trade not in mediation but would like to know your assigned mediator's name, please refer to <a href="../finding-your-mediator/">Finding your mediator</a>.</p>
<span class="wiki-legacy-anchor" id="Level_3:_Arbitration" aria-hidden="true"></span><h2 id="level-3-arbitration"> Level 3: Arbitration </h2>
<figure class="wiki-source-image" id="source-image-3"><a href="../../media/wiki/2caebcdeac6af275a59fadaec67e8a89bcb055e1df579d7a1098b6ecafff5693.png"><img alt="Reject mediator's suggestion in order to start arbitration." decoding="async" height="956" loading="lazy" src="../../media/wiki/2caebcdeac6af275a59fadaec67e8a89bcb055e1df579d7a1098b6ecafff5693.png" width="1750"></a><figcaption>Reject mediator's suggestion in order to start arbitration.<br><small>Historical full-size recovered source. <a href="../../recovered-media/">Provenance catalogue</a>.</small></figcaption></figure>
<p><a href="../arbitration/">Arbitration</a> is the final tier of dispute resolution on Bisq. It's designed to be rare, and its measures are intended for extreme situations. <a href="../arbitration/">Arbitration</a> is only available when:</p>
<ul>
<li>One or both traders reject a <a href="../mediator/">mediator's</a> proposed solution.</li>
<li>The <a href="../arbitration/">time-locked transaction</a> <small class="wiki-link-gap">[section not recovered: Time-Locked Payout Transaction]</small> (the delayed payout transaction created at the trade's start) is published by one of the traders.</li>
</ul>
<p>This transaction sends <strong>all</strong> funds in the multisig escrow (both traders' funds) to the <a href="../burning-men/">Burning Men</a>.</p>
<p>The delayed payout transaction can only be published 10 days after the deposit transaction is confirmed for altcoin trades, or 20 days for fiat trades.</p>
<p>Consider this transaction as a safeguard or fallback option, to be used only when other options have failed.</p>
<aside class="wiki-admonition" role="note"><p><strong>Warning</strong></p><p><a href="../mediation/">Mediation</a> is expected to be the best resolution for both traders. If a trade goes to <a href="../arbitration/">arbitration</a>, the <a href="../arbitrator/">arbitrator</a> may advance a discretionary refund, which is not guaranteed to include the trade amount or a peer's <a href="../security-deposit/">security deposit</a>. The total <a href="../arbitration/">arbitration</a> payout may differ from the <a href="../mediation/">mediation</a> payout. Both traders agreeing to the <a href="../mediator/">mediator's</a> suggestion is the preferred outcome. Requesting <a href="../arbitration/">arbitration</a> should be reserved for exceptional circumstances, such as one peer not responding, or if a trader believes the <a href="../mediator/">mediator</a> did not make a fair payout suggestion.</p>
<p>This dynamic enables a form of mutually assured destruction, driving dispute resolution on Bisq without relying on trusted third parties.</p>
</aside>
<p>Practically, here's how <a href="../arbitration/">arbitration</a> works:</p>
<ol>
<li>If you're trading with an unresponsive peer or feel the <a href="../mediator/">mediator's</a> suggestion was unfair, publish the time-locked transaction as soon as possible and request <a href="../arbitration/">arbitration</a>.</li>
<li>Collaborate with the <a href="../arbitrator/">arbitrator</a> to clarify your case's details.</li>
<li>If the <a href="../arbitrator/">arbitrator</a> sides with you, a refund agent may reimburse you directly, but this is not guaranteed.</li>
<li>The <a href="../arbitrator/">arbitrator</a> will then request reimbursement from the <a href="../dao-user-reference/#bisq-dao">Bisq DAO</a> for the reimbursements they've paid. This isn't something you need to worry about as a trader, but it's good to know how the process works on both sides.</li>
<li><a href="../arbitrator/">Arbitrators</a> are required to respond to messages within 5 days, so it may take a bit longer for them to respond than mediators (you should still respond to messages within 2 days).</li>
</ol>

<span class="wiki-legacy-anchor" id="How_to_find_your_arbitrator_?" aria-hidden="true"></span><h3 id="how-to-find-your-arbitrator"> How to find your arbitrator ?</h3>
<p>Your arbitrator's name should be visible when you open a arbitration ticket. Look for it under the 'support agent' column.</p>
<p>If, however, you have a trade not in arbitration but would like to know your assigned arbitrator's name, please refer to <a href="../finding-your-arbitrator/">Finding your arbitrator</a>.</p>
<span class="wiki-legacy-anchor" id="If_all_else_fails" aria-hidden="true"></span><h2 id="if-all-else-fails"> If all else fails </h2>
<p>One advantage of a flat, decentralized governance structure that encourages user involvement is that no user should ever feel hindered from receiving a fair trade outcome because of a single individual's decision. Bisq isn't a black-box corporation with inscrutable processes. It's an open software project that is governed openly.</p>
<p>Therefore, for whatever reason, if the process above doesn't work for you, you can make a <a href="../making-a-reimbursement-request/">make a reimbursement request</a> yourself in the <a href="../dao-user-reference/#bisq-dao">Bisq DAO</a>. The <a href="../dao-user-reference/#bisq-dao">Bisq DAO</a> <strong>not any single individual is the ultimate arbiter of policy and decision-making for all things Bisq</strong>.</p>
<p>In fact, this is how <a href="../arbitration/">arbitration</a> will eventually be handled anyway.</p>
