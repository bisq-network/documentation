<div class="wiki-recovered-article" lang="en"><p>A <strong>stakeholder</strong> in the Bisq network is any <strong>individual</strong> that holds <a href="../bsq/">BSQ</a> tokens. Ownership of BSQ grants the holder the ability (and responsibility) to participate in the <a href="../introduction-to-the-dao/">Bisq DAO's governance</a>.</p>
<p>Stakeholders can come from various backgrounds – they may be active traders using the platform, contributors developing the software or providing support, or simply individuals who have acquired BSQ and wish to have a say in the project's direction.</p>
<span class="wiki-legacy-anchor" id="What_Can_Stakeholders_Do?" aria-hidden="true"></span><h2 id="what-can-stakeholders-do"> What Can Stakeholders Do? </h2>
<p>The primary function of a stakeholder is to participate in <strong><a href="../introduction-to-the-dao/">DAO governance</a></strong> through voting. Stakeholders use their BSQ voting weight to decide on various types of <a href="../proposals/">Proposals</a>:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Compensation Requests:</strong> Approving payments (in newly issued BSQ) to contributors for work completed.</li>
<li><strong>Reimbursement Requests:</strong> Approving reimbursements (in BSQ) for specific expenses incurred by contributors.</li>
<li><strong>Parameter Change Proposals:</strong> Voting on changes to network or trading parameters (e.g., trading fees, security deposit settings).</li>
<li><strong>Role Proposals:</strong> Approving contributors for specific <a href="../bisq-2-roles/">bonded roles</a>.</li>
<li><strong>Confiscation Proposals:</strong> Voting on whether to confiscate a bonded role holder's BSQ bond due to misconduct.</li>
<li><strong>Generic Proposals:</strong> Voting on broader strategic decisions or other matters affecting the Bisq project.</li>
</ul>
<p>Details on the mechanics of voting, including casting votes, stake weight, and the different phases of a voting cycle, can be found on the <a href="../participating-in-a-dao-voting-cycle/">Participating in a DAO voting cycle</a> page. Note that certain actions, like submitting proposals or casting votes, may require paying small fees in BSQ.</p>
<span class="wiki-legacy-anchor" id="Why_Participate?" aria-hidden="true"></span><h2 id="why-participate"> Why Participate? </h2>
<p>Active stakeholder participation is vital for the health and decentralization of the Bisq network:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Guides Development:</strong> Voting on compensation requests directly funds the work contributors do, influencing what gets built and maintained.</li>
<li><strong>Sets Strategy:</strong> Voting on parameter changes and generic proposals shapes the rules, economics, and future direction of the platform.</li>
<li><strong>Ensures Decentralization:</strong> Wide participation prevents governance power from concentrating in too few hands, upholding Bisq's core principles.</li>
</ul>
<p>By voting, stakeholders collectively steer the Bisq project according to the community's consensus.</p>
