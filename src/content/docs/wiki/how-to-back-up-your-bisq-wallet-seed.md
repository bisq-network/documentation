<div class="wiki-recovered-article" lang="en"><p>Download, install Bisq.</p>&#10;<p>1. Go to <strong>Account</strong> tab.</p>&#10;<p>2. Go to <strong>Wallet Seed</strong> tab.</p>&#10;<p>3. Write down on paper the <strong>12 words</strong> and the <strong>wallet date</strong>.</p>&#10;<p>4. These 12 words and date can be used to restore your wallet if you lose access to Bisq.</p>&#10;<p>NEVER GIVE THEM TO ANYBODY. Your wallet seed controls your keys, your keys control your Bitcoin. Don't keep them online. Don't send them online. Don't take a digital picture of them. Keep them offline. Remember - <strong>NOT YOUR KEYS, NOT YOUR BITCOIN!</strong></p>&#10;<p>Bisq has no access to your Bitcoin or your private data. It is your personal responsability to control and back up your funds.</p>&#10;<ul>&#10;<li>For more short guides see <a href="../howto/">Howto</a>. You can also find support on <strong><a href="https://bisq.chat" rel="noopener noreferrer">Matrix</a></strong>.</li>&#10;</ul>&#10;</div>
