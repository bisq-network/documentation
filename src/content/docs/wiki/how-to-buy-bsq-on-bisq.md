<div class="wiki-recovered-article" lang="en"><p>First download, install Bisq and create a backup (copy seed words - see <a href="../how-to-back-up-your-bisq-wallet-seed/">How to back up your Bisq wallet seed</a>).</p>&#10;<p>Now you can trade. To buy your first BSQ follow these simple steps:</p>&#10;<p>1. On <strong>Market</strong> tab select BSQ/BTC</p>&#10;<p>2. Click on <strong>BUY BSQ</strong></p>&#10;<p>3. Select an offer and click on <strong>TAKE OFFER</strong></p>&#10;<p>4. Review offer details including amount, price. Then click on NEXT STEP.</p>&#10;<p>5. Fund your trade (from Bisq wallet or external wallet).</p>&#10;<p>6. Click on <strong>CONFIRM TAKE OFFER</strong>.</p>&#10;<p>7. BSQ/BTC swap is now complete. Wait for blockchain confirmation.</p>&#10;<p>8. Check that you have received BSQ in your wallet.</p>&#10;<p>BSQ/BTC swaps are <strong>Atomic Swaps</strong>. This means that they are either completed or not. They cannot be stuck or incomplete. They have no counterparty risk and require no <strong>trusted third party</strong> to be involved.</p>&#10;<ul>&#10;<li>For more short guides see <a href="../howto/">Howto</a>. You can also find support on <strong><a href="https://bisq.chat" rel="noopener noreferrer">Matrix</a></strong>.</li>&#10;</ul>&#10;</div>
