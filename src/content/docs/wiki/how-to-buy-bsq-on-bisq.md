<div class="wiki-recovered-article" lang="en"><p>First download, install Bisq and create a backup (copy seed words - see <a href="../how-to-back-up-your-bisq-wallet-seed/">How to back up your Bisq wallet seed</a>).</p>
<p>Now you can trade. To buy your first BSQ follow these simple steps:</p>
<p>1. On <strong>Market</strong> tab select BSQ/BTC</p>
<p>2. Click on <strong>BUY BSQ</strong></p>
<p>3. Select an offer and click on <strong>TAKE OFFER</strong></p>
<p>4. Review offer details including amount, price. Then click on NEXT STEP.</p>
<p>5. Fund your trade (from Bisq wallet or external wallet).</p>
<p>6. Click on <strong>CONFIRM TAKE OFFER</strong>.</p>
<p>7. BSQ/BTC swap is now complete. Wait for blockchain confirmation.</p>
<p>8. Check that you have received BSQ in your wallet.</p>
<p>BSQ/BTC swaps are <strong>Atomic Swaps</strong>. Both assets are exchanged in the same Bitcoin transaction, so one side cannot settle on its own. Broadcasting can fail and confirmation can be delayed; wait for the transaction's confirmation and check the received asset. The exchange does not require a trusted third party.</p>
