First download, install Bisq and create a backup (copy seed words - see [How to back up your Bisq wallet seed](../how-to-back-up-your-bisq-wallet-seed/)).

Now you can trade. To buy your first BSQ follow these simple steps:

1\. On **Market** tab select BSQ/BTC

2\. Click on **BUY BSQ**

3\. Select an offer and click on **TAKE OFFER**

4\. Review offer details including amount, price. Then click on NEXT STEP.

5\. Fund your trade (from Bisq wallet or external wallet).

6\. Click on **CONFIRM TAKE OFFER**.

7\. BSQ/BTC swap is now complete. Wait for blockchain confirmation.

8\. Check that you have received BSQ in your wallet.

BSQ/BTC swaps are **Atomic Swaps**. Both assets are exchanged in the same Bitcoin transaction, so one side cannot settle on its own. Broadcasting can fail and confirmation can be delayed; wait for the transaction\'s confirmation and check the received asset. The exchange does not require a trusted third party.
