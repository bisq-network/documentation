**Trading fees** are paid to fund the <span class="wiki-link-gap"> Bisq DAO <small>[unrecovered article: DAO]</small></span> when making or taking an offer. Fees can be paid in BSQ or BTC.

<span class="wiki-legacy-anchor" id="Getting_a_discount" aria-hidden="true">
</span>

## Getting a discount {#getting-a-discount}

Bisq encourages traders to use [BSQ](../bsq/) by offering a discount of approximately 25%. This discount fluctuates as the BSQ/BTC rate fluctuates. To maintain the 25% discount target, trading fees are <span class="wiki-link-gap"> updated <small>[unrecovered article: update BSQ trading fees]</small></span> every cycle as necessary.

<aside class="wiki-admonition" role="note">

**Note**

If you\'re interested in using BSQ to pay trading fees, [see this article for instructions](../paying-trading-fees-with-bsq/#acquire-bsq).

</aside>

Combined BTC trading fee rate is 1.3% (0.15% by maker and 1.15% by taker). Absolute minimum fee to avoid dust is 0.000075 BTC.

Combined BSQ trading fee rate is targeted at 0.975% (0.1125% by maker and 0.8625% by taker). Absolute minimum fee to avoid dust is 0.03 BSQ.

Trading fee rates and absolute minimums are DAO parameters that [must be proposed](../proposals/) and approved through DAO voting in order to take effect. BTC fee rates tend to stay constant, but BSQ rates are [adjusted every cycle](../updating-bsq-trading-fees/) to account for BSQ rate fluctuation.
