<div class="wiki-recovered-article" lang="en"><p><strong>Trading fees</strong> are paid to fund the <span class="wiki-link-gap"> Bisq DAO <small>[unrecovered article: DAO]</small></span> when making or taking an offer. Fees can be paid in BSQ or BTC.</p>&#10;<h2 id="getting-a-discount"> Getting a discount </h2>&#10;<p>Bisq encourages traders to use <a href="../bsq/">BSQ</a> by offering a discount of approximately 25%. This discount fluctuates as the BSQ/BTC rate fluctuates. To maintain the 25% discount target, trading fees are <span class="wiki-link-gap"> updated <small>[unrecovered article: update BSQ trading fees]</small></span> every cycle as necessary.</p>&#10;<aside class="wiki-admonition" role="note"><p><strong>Note</strong></p><p>If you're interested in using BSQ to pay trading fees, <a href="../paying-trading-fees-with-bsq/#acquire-bsq">see this article for instructions</a>.</p>&#10;</aside>&#10;<h2 id="current-rates"> Current rates </h2>&#10;<table>&#10;<caption>Trading Fees per 1 BTC</caption>&#10;<thead>&#10;<tr>&#10;<th></th>&#10;<th>BTC</th>&#10;<th>BSQ</th>&#10;</tr>&#10;</thead>&#10;<tbody>&#10;<tr>&#10;<td>Maker</td>&#10;<td>0.0015</td>&#10;<td>64.95</td>&#10;</tr>&#10;<tr>&#10;<td>Taker</td>&#10;<td>0.0115</td>&#10;<td>497.95</td>&#10;</tr>&#10;</tbody>&#10;</table>&#10;<details class="wiki-table-source"><summary>Original table source</summary><pre><code>{| class="wikitable" style="width: 250px;"&#10;|+ Trading Fees per 1 BTC&#10;|-&#10;!&#10;!BTC&#10;!BSQ&#10;|-&#10;!Maker&#10;|0.0015&#10;|64.95&#10;|-&#10;!Taker&#10;|0.0115&#10;|497.95&#10;|}</code></pre></details>&#10;<p>Combined BTC trading fee rate is 1.3% (0.15% by maker and 1.15% by taker). Absolute minimum fee to avoid dust is 0.000075 BTC.</p>&#10;<p>Combined BSQ trading fee rate is targeted at 0.975% (0.1125% by maker and 0.8625% by taker). Absolute minimum fee to avoid dust is 0.03 BSQ.</p>&#10;<p>Trading fee rates and absolute minimums are DAO parameters that <a href="../proposals/">must be proposed</a> and approved through DAO voting in order to take effect. BTC fee rates tend to stay constant, but BSQ rates are <a href="../updating-bsq-trading-fees/">adjusted every cycle</a> to account for BSQ rate fluctuation.</p>&#10;<h2 id="selecting-how-to-pay"> Selecting how to pay </h2>&#10;<p>Traders choose how they'd prefer to pay trading fees while making or taking an offer. BSQ and BTC amounts (and fiat equivalents) are shown.</p>&#10;<figure class="wiki-source-image" id="source-image-1"><a href="../../media/wiki/47c712c2701a0c712b2c1edd8a22a906e9f47e560cf2d81c255774ae959ae50b.png"><img alt="Pick how to pay trade fees on this screen while making or taking an offer." decoding="async" height="710" loading="lazy" src="../../media/wiki/47c712c2701a0c712b2c1edd8a22a906e9f47e560cf2d81c255774ae959ae50b.png" width="1200"></a><figcaption>Pick how to pay trade fees on this screen while making or taking an offer.<br><small>Historical full-size recovered source. <a href="../../recovered-media/">Provenance catalogue</a>.</small></figcaption></figure>&#10;<div>&#10;</div>&#10;</div>
