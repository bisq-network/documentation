<div class="wiki-recovered-article" lang="en"><p><strong>Trading fees</strong> are paid to fund the <span class="wiki-link-gap"> Bisq DAO <small>[unrecovered article: DAO]</small></span> when making or taking an offer. Fees can be paid in BSQ or BTC.</p>
<span class="wiki-legacy-anchor" id="Getting_a_discount" aria-hidden="true"></span><h2 id="getting-a-discount"> Getting a discount </h2>
<p>Bisq encourages traders to use <a href="../bsq/">BSQ</a> by offering a discount of approximately 25%. This discount fluctuates as the BSQ/BTC rate fluctuates. To maintain the 25% discount target, trading fees are <span class="wiki-link-gap"> updated <small>[unrecovered article: update BSQ trading fees]</small></span> every cycle as necessary.</p>
<aside class="wiki-admonition" role="note"><p><strong>Note</strong></p><p>If you're interested in using BSQ to pay trading fees, <a href="../paying-trading-fees-with-bsq/#acquire-bsq">see this article for instructions</a>.</p>
</aside>

<p>Combined BTC trading fee rate is 1.3% (0.15% by maker and 1.15% by taker). Absolute minimum fee to avoid dust is 0.000075 BTC.</p>
<p>Combined BSQ trading fee rate is targeted at 0.975% (0.1125% by maker and 0.8625% by taker). Absolute minimum fee to avoid dust is 0.03 BSQ.</p>
<p>Trading fee rates and absolute minimums are DAO parameters that <a href="../proposals/">must be proposed</a> and approved through DAO voting in order to take effect. BTC fee rates tend to stay constant, but BSQ rates are <a href="../updating-bsq-trading-fees/">adjusted every cycle</a> to account for BSQ rate fluctuation.</p>
