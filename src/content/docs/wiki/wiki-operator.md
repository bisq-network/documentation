## Duties {#duties}

- Owning and renewing the bisq.wiki domain name
- Administering bisq.wiki DNS records
- Operating and administering the Mediawiki instance at bisq.wiki, including:
  - Adding and maintaining plugins
  - Daily Backups
    - Full MySQL database dump
    - Full filesystem archive of webroot
    - Off-site mirror of backup
- Triaging incoming issues to the bisq-network/wiki repository
