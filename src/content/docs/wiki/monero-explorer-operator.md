## Duties {#duties}

- Run an instance of the [Onion Monero Blockchain Explorer](https://github.com/moneroexamples/onion-monero-blockchain-explorer)
- Make your Monero Explorer instance available over HTTPS clearnet and Tor V3 HS onion, enable HSTS and DNSSEC
- Update the [Bisq code](https://github.com/bisq-network/bisq/blob/master/core/src/main/java/bisq/core/user/Preferences.java#L130) to contain the FQDN clearnet hostname and Tor V3 HS onion of your Monero Explorer
