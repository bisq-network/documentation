<div class="wiki-recovered-article" lang="en"><p>It is critical that you <strong>back up your wallet seed</strong>. The Bisq wallet is totally controlled by you, and you only—wallets are created and stored by the Bisq software right on your machine.</p>
<p>For this reason, you should keep your Bisq wallet's seed words safe and <a href="../encrypting-your-wallet/">encrypt your Bisq wallet</a> as well. There are no other copies of this data, so you're on your own if you lose them.</p>
<span class="wiki-legacy-anchor" id="Write_down_your_seed_words" aria-hidden="true"></span><h2 id="write-down-your-seed-words"> Write down your seed words </h2>
<p>With your wallet seed words and date, you can recover your Bisq wallet and funds if the wallet is ever lost, destroyed, or corrupted.</p>
<span class="wiki-template-gap" role="note"><strong>[Unresolved template: Wallet_date_explainer]</strong> <code>{{Wallet_date_explainer}}</code></span>
<p>You can find your wallet's seed words in <code class="wiki-source-code" id="source-code-1">Account</code> &gt; <code class="wiki-source-code" id="source-code-2">Wallet seed</code>. If you've encrypted your wallet, you'll need to enter the password before you can see your seed words.</p>
<p><strong>Please <em>actually</em> write down your seed words and date with a pen and paper.</strong> Saving them on your computer, even if you do so in a secure password manager, makes it possible for a hacker to obtain them through sneaky schemes like keylogging. Going low-tech with pen and paper eliminates all such digital attack vectors.</p>

<span class="wiki-legacy-anchor" id="What_seed_words_do_and_don&#x27;t_do" aria-hidden="true"></span><h2 id="what-seed-words-do-and-don-t-do"> What seed words do and don't do </h2>
<p>Backing up a Bisq wallet's seed words only ensures that you will always have access to the funds stored in that Bisq wallet.</p>
<p><strong>All other Bisq data must be backed up separately.</strong> Keys used for signing P2P messages (trade-related messages such as acknowledging payment sending/receiving, trader chat, dispute resolution, etc), Tor keys, payment account details, and more must be backed up separately.</p>
