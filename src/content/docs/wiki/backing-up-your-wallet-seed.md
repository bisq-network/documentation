<div class="wiki-recovered-article" lang="en"><p>It is critical that you <strong>back up your wallet seed</strong>. The Bisq wallet is totally controlled by you, and you only—wallets are created and stored by the Bisq software right on your machine.</p>&#10;<p>For this reason, you should keep your Bisq wallet's seed words safe and <a href="../encrypting-your-wallet/">encrypt your Bisq wallet</a> as well. There are no other copies of this data, so you're on your own if you lose them.</p>&#10;<h2 id="write-down-your-seed-words"> Write down your seed words </h2>&#10;<p>With your wallet seed words and date, you can recover your Bisq wallet and funds if the wallet is ever lost, destroyed, or corrupted.</p>&#10;<span class="wiki-template-gap" role="note"><strong>[Unresolved template: Wallet_date_explainer]</strong> <code>{{Wallet_date_explainer}}</code></span>&#10;<p>You can find your wallet's seed words in <code class="wiki-source-code" id="source-code-1">Account</code> &gt; <code class="wiki-source-code" id="source-code-2">Wallet seed</code>. If you've encrypted your wallet, you'll need to enter the password before you can see your seed words.</p>&#10;<p><strong>Please <em>actually</em> write down your seed words and date with a pen and paper.</strong> Saving them on your computer, even if you do so in a secure password manager, makes it possible for a hacker to obtain them through sneaky schemes like keylogging. Going low-tech with pen and paper eliminates all such digital attack vectors.</p>&#10;<figure class="wiki-source-image" id="source-image-1"><a href="../../media/wiki/d2ea84b7c6e2a35395099a9afe80611c07d3b159ab64954ffacf6787f9d76d42.png"><img alt="Location of wallet seed words and date." decoding="async" height="741" loading="lazy" src="../../media/wiki/d2ea84b7c6e2a35395099a9afe80611c07d3b159ab64954ffacf6787f9d76d42.png" width="1200"></a><figcaption>Location of wallet seed words and date.<br><small>Historical full-size recovered source. <a href="../../recovered-media/">Provenance catalogue</a>.</small></figcaption></figure>&#10;<div>&#10;</div>&#10;<h2 id="what-seed-words-do-and-don-t-do"> What seed words do and don't do </h2>&#10;<p>Backing up a Bisq wallet's seed words only ensures that you will always have access to the funds stored in that Bisq wallet.</p>&#10;<p><strong>All other Bisq data must be backed up separately.</strong> Keys used for signing P2P messages (trade-related messages such as acknowledging payment sending/receiving, trader chat, dispute resolution, etc), Tor keys, payment account details, and more must be backed up separately.</p>&#10;<h2 id="wallet-derivation-paths"> Wallet derivation paths </h2>&#10;<span class="wiki-template-gap" role="note"><strong>[Unresolved template: Wallet derivation paths]</strong> <code>{{Wallet derivation paths}}</code></span>&#10;</div>
