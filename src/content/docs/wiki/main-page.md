Bisq is a decentralized <span class="wiki-link-gap">peer-to-peer <small>[unrecovered article: P2P Network]</small></span> bitcoin exchange network that you access via software running on your own computer. It enables secure, private, and censorship-resistant trading directly with other users, without relying on intermediaries.

Bisq offers two software versions providing different trading experiences:

<span class="wiki-legacy-anchor" id="Bisq_1" aria-hidden="true">
</span>

## Bisq 1 {#bisq-1}

[Bisq 1](../bisq-1/) is the original, well-established peer-to-peer exchange platform launched in 2016 (initially as BitSquare). It allows users to trade Bitcoin for fiat currencies and various altcoins using a robust [multisig deposit system](../security-deposit/) for security between strangers. Individual trade sizes on Bisq 1 can reach up to <span class="wiki-template-gap" role="note"><strong>[Unresolved template: MaxTradeSizeBisq1]</strong> <code>{{MaxTradeSizeBisq1}}</code></span> for fiat trades and <span class="wiki-template-gap" role="note"><strong>[Unresolved template: MaxTradeSizeAltcoin]</strong> <code>{{MaxTradeSizeAltcoin}}</code></span> for altcoin trades.

**[Learn more about Bisq 1](../bisq-1/)**

## Bisq 2 {#bisq-2}

Launched in 2024, [Bisq 2](../bisq-2/) is the next-generation platform designed to support multiple [trade protocols](../trade-protocols/). The first, and currently primary, protocol available is [Bisq Easy](../bisq-easy/).

[Bisq Easy](../bisq-easy/) facilitates peer-to-peer Bitcoin-fiat trades with security based on seller [reputation](../reputation/), rather than deposits. It features no trading fees and no deposit requirements, making it accessible for smaller trades up to the equivalent of 600 USD (or <span class="wiki-template-gap" role="note"><strong>[Unresolved template: MaxBisqEasyTrade]</strong> <code>{{MaxBisqEasyTrade}}</code></span>). It is positioned as a simpler alternative for users, especially those new to Bisq or making smaller fiat trades.

**[Learn more about Bisq 2 & Bisq Easy](../bisq-2/)**
