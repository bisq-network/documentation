### So, how do I send fiat to Bisq, or how do I use my credit card to buy bitcoin? {#so-how-do-i-send-fiat-to-bisq-or-how-do-i-use-my-credit-card-to-buy-bitcoin}

<span class="wiki-legacy-anchor" id="You_don&#x27;t." aria-hidden="true">
</span>

## You don\'t. {#you-don-t}

This guide was made as an attempt to garner attention from such query terms that regularly appear on some of our support venues.

Bisq is a DEX (decentralized exchange), it doesn\'t directly deal with fiat, so it doesn\'t have builtin fiat accounts, nor credit card payment endpoints; it only manages a BTC wallet (and a special [BSQ](../bsq/) wallet).

To trade, you need to find another user who agrees with you (selling BTC while you want to buy, or buying BTC while you want to sell, using same currency and same [payment method](../payment-methods/)); Bisq will manage the Bitcoin side of the transaction with its battle-tested escrow, and trading peers will manage the fiat/altcoin side on their end, by dealing directly between themselves.

#### There might be a way to buy BTC with credit card on Bisq though {#there-might-be-a-way-to-buy-btc-with-credit-card-on-bisq-though}

Technically, you could say you can buy BTC with your credit card on Bisq, even if that\'s a little more convoluted than what you normally see on e-commerce websites: you would need to setup a payment method that supports receiving deposits from cards, like [Revolut](../revolut/), <span class="wiki-link-gap">Wise <small>[unrecovered article: TransferWise]</small></span> (ex TransferWise), [Amazon eGift card](../amazon-egift-card/) to say a few, send money from your credit card to those accounts, and then make, or take, an offer that uses one of those methods.
