*Bisq 2 v2.1.15 and Bisq 1 v1.10.11 · Checked 7 November 2026.*

**Buying your first bitcoin? Start by exploring Bisq Easy in Bisq 2.** It lets you buy without already owning bitcoin for a security deposit. Read how seller reputation works before accepting an offer.

**Already using bitcoin and looking for multisig trading? Explore Bisq 1.** You need bitcoin for its deposit and transaction costs before trading. Bisq 1 also provides the DAO interface.

## Compare the trading methods

| | Bisq Easy in Bisq 2 | Multisig trading in Bisq 1 |
|---|---|---|
| Protection | The buyer relies on the seller's reputation and pays first. | Both traders lock security deposits; the traded bitcoin is held in a 2-of-2 multisig transaction. |
| Bitcoin needed before buying | No security deposit required. | Bitcoin is needed for the deposit and applicable fees. |
| Costs | No Bisq Easy trading fee. The offer can include a price premium; Bitcoin delivery can incur network costs. | Trading and mining fees apply. Check the final amount shown by the app. |
| If something goes wrong | In-app mediation can help the parties reach agreement; it cannot force a payment. | Trade chat and mediation are the starting points, followed by the applicable dispute process. |
| First step | [Install Bisq 2](../../bisq-2/install-and-verify/), then [make a first trade](../../bisq-2/first-trade/). | [Install Bisq 1](../../bisq-1/install-and-verify/), then [prepare a first trade](../../bisq-1/first-trade/). |

Reputation and deposits reduce different risks. Neither guarantees that a trade will succeed. Read the offer's payment method, limits, deadlines and terms before accepting it.

## Keep the apps and protocols distinct

Bisq 1 and Bisq 2 are separate applications and can run alongside each other. Installing Bisq 2 does not migrate your Bisq 1 wallet, trades or backups.

These beginner guides cover **Bisq Easy** in Bisq 2 and **multisig trading** in Bisq 1. Other or experimental protocols have different rules; do not apply the Bisq Easy payment or recovery instructions to them.

Back up the app you use: [Bisq 2 backup and recovery](../../bisq-2/backup-and-recovery/) or [Bisq 1 backup and recovery](../../bisq-1/backup-and-recovery/). If you already have an open trade, stay with its application and use the corresponding [Bisq 2 help](../../bisq-2/get-help/) or [Bisq 1 dispute guide](../../bisq-1/dispute-help/).

## Where the DAO fits

The [Bisq DAO](../../dao/overview/) coordinates funding and governance. You do not need to become a DAO contributor to start trading. If you want to help improve Bisq, begin with [contributing](../../contribute/getting-started/).
