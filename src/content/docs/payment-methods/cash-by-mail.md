*Checked 7 November 2026 · Bisq 1 v1.10.11.*

Cash by mail is a currently registered Bisq 1 method with an **eight-day** trade period. It is unusually difficult to prove what was packed, delivered or received. Postal loss, theft and disputed contents can leave either party without an effective remedy.

## Define the account and terms

The account records a name/nym, address, currency and additional information. Use one currency per account and consider separate buying/selling accounts for different instructions. The seller needs a receiving address; the buyer needs a valid return address. A PO box can reduce home-address exposure if it is lawful and usable for the chosen service.

The offer's information icon exposes special conditions before acceptance. Read them before taking the offer; if you cannot meet them, make a different offer. Do not advertise off-platform contact details in additional information.

## Prepare before accepting

Arrange tamper-evident packaging, an appropriate tracked courier and any agreed insurance first. Check whether the carrier permits cash and actually insures that content; the old $50,000 insurance example is not a current promise.

Put sealed cash inside inconspicuous protective packaging, rather than an envelope that clearly reveals banknotes. Address inner and outer packaging consistently, include a return address and seal it securely. Follow the agreed terms and applicable carrier rules.

## Keep evidence of the entire shipment

Record clear photos or preferably a continuous video of the cash, packing, seal, address and already-attached tracking label. Retain handover/tracking evidence. The recipient should record opening the package with the matching tracking number visible. Keep this evidence private for the trade/dispute.

Delivery confirmation establishes delivery, not the contents. Count and validate the cash before confirming receipt in Bisq. Do not release BTC solely because a parcel was marked delivered.

## Understand the dispute risk

Use trader chat early. Mediation can suggest a resolution but cannot reconstruct an unrecorded handover. The old source said rejection automatically donates both peers' funds and promised final DAO exclusions; this guide does not promote that shorthand into a current payout rule. Follow the current [Bisq 1 dispute process](../../bisq-1/dispute-help/) and its discretionary-reimbursement limits.

Cash-by-mail evidence can still be inconclusive. Do not treat arbitration, insurance or a DAO request as guaranteed recovery.
