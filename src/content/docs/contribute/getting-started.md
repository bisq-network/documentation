*Project guidance · Checked 7 November 2026.*

Start with one useful task and contact the maintainers who own it. Documentation, testing, support, translation and code all need people who can explain their work and respond to review.

## Check priorities before starting

**Bisq is temporarily pausing new contributors for compensated development.** Bisq's [4 November 2026 update](https://bisq.network/blog/open-source-development-in-the-age-of-ai/) also pauses routine review of new contributors' development pull requests. Maintainers may make exceptions, but neither review nor compensation is guaranteed. Discuss the task, review capacity and any funding before investing substantial time.

Join the [Bisq Matrix community](https://bisq.chat) and find the relevant project or team. For code, start in the repository that owns the feature: [Bisq 1](https://github.com/bisq-network/bisq) and [Bisq 2](https://github.com/bisq-network/bisq2) have separate build and contribution instructions.

## Make the change easy to review

1. Describe the problem and the intended result. Check that someone else is not already working on it.
2. Agree a bounded scope with the maintainer. Keep unrelated cleanup out of the change.
3. Follow the target repository's instructions. Bisq 1's contribution guide calls for signed commits and focused pull requests; check the rules of other repositories individually.
4. Submit the work with the reason for the change and evidence that it works. State what you tested and what remains untested.
5. Respond to feedback and take responsibility for maintaining your contribution.

When using AI, inspect the output and verify its claims. A polished draft still needs an author who understands the change. Do not include credentials, private messages, wallet secrets or users' payment details in public review material.

## Review this documentation

Choose a guide you know well and compare it with the application you use. Report the page, product version, incorrect or unclear step, and a supporting source. The [review page](../../review/) lists the current batch and known gaps. Open an [issue in this preview repository](https://github.com/hiciefte/bisq-docs/issues) or propose a focused correction there.

## Discuss compensation separately

Compensation uses the [Bisq DAO](../../dao/overview/). Eligible completed work can be submitted through the [compensation repository](https://github.com/bisq-network/compensation), following the current process and cycle. Prior agreement, a merged change or a useful contribution does not by itself guarantee a successful DAO vote.
