This is a **community documentation preview**, maintained in [hiciefte/bisq-docs](https://github.com/hiciefte/bisq-docs). Human maintainer review is still needed before it becomes an official replacement.

## What changed

The recovered wiki topics now have homes in the main documentation: Bisq 1, Bisq 2, payment methods, DAO, contributing, and reference. Overlapping articles are consolidated, and useful technical detail and historical images are retained in the relevant subject guides. There is no separate historical wiki reading section.

Search includes these guides, including mediator names such as **suddenwhipvapor** and **luis3672**. A name being searchable does not establish current availability; the mediator guide explains the role evidence and how to identify the agent assigned to a trade.

Old exported wiki paths, such as `/Finding_your_mediator`, lead to the corresponding guide. The [title index](../browse/all/#former-wiki-titles) maps all 209 recovered titles. Existing `/wiki/…/` preview links remain usable too.

## What was checked

Guides identify their checked sources and version context. The current application baseline is **Bisq 1 v1.10.11** and **Bisq 2 v2.1.15**. Checks compare claims with official release material, application strings and implementation, public role records, and relevant primary provider documentation.

Current instructions, version-specific reference and unresolved facts are distinguished in the guides. An old screenshot, role listing or provider description is not evidence that the same behavior or availability continues today. Source checks do not certify that funded trades, installer verification, wallet restores, or DAO actions were executed.

## Feedback we need

Please check:

- **Accuracy:** incorrect limits, payment steps, recovery instructions, role information or product boundaries.
- **Coverage:** useful explanations, commands or images missing from the integrated guide.
- **Findability:** unsuccessful searches, confusing navigation or a saved wiki link that does not reach the right topic.
- **Clarity:** unnecessary repetition, ambiguous prerequisites or warnings separated from the action they concern.

[Report a correction](https://github.com/hiciefte/bisq-docs/issues) with the page link, product/version, suggested change and supporting source or reproducible observation. Never include wallet seeds, private keys, payment details or private trade conversations in public issues.

## Source preservation and remaining gaps

Expandable source-history sections link to original article text and available revision metadata. The guides use 99 recovered images in context. The [media catalogue](../recovered-media/) also retains 21 redundant or obsolete illustrations, preserving all 120 recovered files. Of those files, 23 are thumbnails; three additional referenced images remain missing. Full authorship history, some source dependencies, and content/media reuse terms remain unresolved. See [recovery scope](../recovery-status/).

The original export is preserved for comparison. Integration does not turn every historical statement into current advice, recover missing source material, or imply control of the old wiki domain. Translations and official adoption should follow contributor review.
