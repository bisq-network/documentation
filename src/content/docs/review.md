These articles are the Bisq wiki as it was recovered in October 2026. Some instructions are out of date, and some topics were never written up. With a GitHub account you can report what is wrong or propose an article the wiki never had. A documentation reviewer accepts every wording change before it is published.

On an article, **Suggest an edit** does the same with the article filled in. **Describe the fix** opens the correction form with the article's address, the section you are reading and any text you selected. **Edit it myself** opens the article in GitHub's editor, where you change the wording and propose it as a pull request. When you select text in an article, **Suggest a fix** appears next to it and opens the form with that text quoted.

Issues and pull requests are public and stay visible. Do not include wallet seeds, private keys, payment details or private trade conversations.

## What happens next

A reviewer or maintainer reads each report. A correction that holds up becomes a pull request that names the report. A pull request that changes an article is labelled and checked automatically; a documentation reviewer then accepts the final wording and approves it, and a maintainer merges it. Merging publishes the change, and the article names it with a link to its pull request. The [contributor guide](https://github.com/bisq-network/documentation/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md#how-a-change-reaches-the-site) explains each step.

## What to review

Read an article alongside the current application and official sources. Please identify outdated instructions, missing warnings, incorrect product boundaries, unclear steps, and images that no longer match the interface. Use particular care with payment, wallet recovery and dispute instructions.

A useful report names the product and version you checked, quotes the current sentence, gives the wording you would use, and links a supporting source or describes what you saw in the application. Never make a funded trade or an irreversible change, such as restoring a wallet, just to check an article.

A separate AI-edited edition and a comparison index are preserved in the repository under `review/`. The 98 edited guides are review material; they are not the main documentation or part of site search. The index helps reviewers compare them with their recovered sources. Scripted checks compare preserved article sources and their rendered counterparts; they do not establish that the historical instructions remain correct today.

## Which Bisq is this about?

Article pages show a Bisq 1 or Bisq 2 label only where the article names that product in its title, a section heading or its migration grouping. An article without a label may concern either product, and a label does not mean that the instructions are current. The [browse page](../browse/all/) can filter articles by the product they name.

## Sources and saved links

Each original article has preserved source text and available revision metadata. All recovered wiki title paths and 98 earlier edited-guide paths remain available as compatibility links. Where an edited guide combined multiple articles, its compatibility page offers the relevant originals for selection.

The [recovery status](../recovery-status/) describes the snapshot, recovered images, missing files, attribution and reuse questions. Old images and role information may no longer reflect current software or availability.
