This is **a community documentation preview**, maintained in [hiciefte/bisq-docs](https://github.com/hiciefte/bisq-docs). It is a proposal for contributors to review, not an official replacement for Bisq's documentation.

The aim is simple: help people choose the right guide, complete a task safely, and find supporting sources without reading repeated explanations.

Each guide has a short sources section. Checks compare documentation with release notes, application text and relevant implementation. They do not certify that a funded trade, installer, wallet restore or DAO action was executed successfully.

## What to look for

Read a guide as someone trying to complete its task. Check that:

- The product, protocol and prerequisites are clear.
- The steps match the supported application and leave out no important warning.
- Links and images help at the point where they appear.
- The guide gives the needed answer without repeating other pages.

[Report a correction](https://github.com/hiciefte/bisq-docs/issues) with the page link, product/version, suggested change and a source or reproducible observation. Share only non-sensitive evidence. Never include wallet seeds, private keys, payment details or private trade conversations in public issues.

## Historical articles remain available

The [wiki archive](../browse/) preserves **209 exported articles** from the snapshot captured on **18 October 2026**. The newest exported article revision is **15 October 2026**. These pages retain their source text and revision information; they are not all rewritten or checked against current releases.

Where a new guide covers an archived topic, the archive points to it. Main search focuses on the revised guides. Use the [complete title index](../browse/all/) to find historical material.

The [media catalogue](../recovered-media/) retains 120 recovered files. Some are thumbnails, and screenshots can show an older interface. Three images remain unavailable. Missing templates and unresolved source links are marked in the archive. Detailed recovery evidence is collected on the [recovery-status page](../recovery-status/).

## What remains before an official replacement

Contributors still need to review the rewritten guides and prioritize the remaining archive. Payment-method specifics, advanced recovery, specialized installation environments, DAO procedures and contributor roles need further focused batches. New translations should follow a reviewed source revision.

The recovered export contains only one revision per article, so full authorship history and content/media reuse terms remain unresolved. Attribution and permission questions are recorded separately from technical correctness; preserving a source does not establish its licence. No official endorsement or migration of the old wiki domain is implied by this preview.
