<div class="wiki-recovered-article" lang="en"><p><strong>Altcoins Instant</strong> is a type of <a href="../payment-methods/">payment account</a> on Bisq that uses cryptocurrencies to settle BTC trades <strong>within 1 hour</strong>—meaning the buyer must send payment and the seller must acknowledge it within 1 hour.</p>
<p>Ordinary Altcoins accounts have a trade period of 1 day. Altcoins Instant payment accounts are identical to ordinary <a href="../altcoins/">Altcoins</a> payment accounts in every other way aside from their trade period.</p>
<p>Only use this payment method for making or taking offers if you know you'll on your machine while the offer is available. Disable it other times to avoid disputes.</p>
<p>See <a href="../creating-a-payment-account/#creating-an-altcoin-account">how to create an Altcoins Instant payment account</a>.</p>
