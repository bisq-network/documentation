<div class="wiki-recovered-article" lang="en"><p><strong>Paxum</strong> is a payment method to due to be added shortly after v1.7.3.</p>
<p>Paxum is a Global Payment Service for Businesses and Individuals. Paxum provides payment services for businesses in higher risk industries such as adult work and affiliate marketing. Paxum provides it's users with virtual payment accounts. For example SEPA, ACH and Faster Payment accounts are provided for the currencies EUR, USD, and GBP.</p>
<p>Paxum to Paxum transfers are instant.</p>
<ul>
<li>Currencies accepted; USD, CAD, EUR, DKK, CZK, AUD, ZAR, THB, CHF, SEK, RON, PLN, NZD, NOK, INR, IDR, HUF, GBP</li>
<li>Trade window: 24 hours</li>
<li>Signing required: No</li>

<span class="wiki-legacy-anchor" id="How_do_I_add_Paxum_as_a_payment_method_in_Bisq?" aria-hidden="true"></span><h2 id="how-do-i-add-paxum-as-a-payment-method-in-bisq"> How do I add Paxum as a payment method in Bisq? </h2>
<p>It is easy to add your Paxum account to Bisq.</p>
<ol>
<li>Go to the top menu in Bisq and select Account.</li>
<li>Then select National Currency Accounts.</li>
<li>Click add new account</li>
<li>Select Paxum</li>
<li>Complete the details</li>
<li>Choose to use a custom account name this helps makes it easier for you to manage multiple payment methods in Bisq (this will be visible only to you, for example: Paxum - John Smith)</li>
<li>Click save new account</li>
</ol>
<p>Make sure all the details are correct when you add.</p>
<span class="wiki-legacy-anchor" id="How_do_I_buy_BTC_with_my_Paxum_account?" aria-hidden="true"></span><h2 id="how-do-i-buy-btc-with-my-paxum-account"> How do I buy BTC with my Paxum account? </h2>
<p>You can buy BTC with Paxum on Bisq in 2 waysː</p>
<ol>
<li>Make an offer to buy BTC on the EUR market.</li>
<li>Take an offer to buy BTC on the EUR market.</li>
</ol>
<p>Payment can be sent to other users with just an email address.</p>
<p>When the offer is confirmed Bisq will provide you with the BTC Sellers Paxum details for you to complete the payment. You should send payment immediately and confirm you have done so in Bisq by clicking 'payment started'.</p>
<p>If you have the option to include a payment reference please leave it blank. If your bank requires one, please just use your name that matches your bank account.</p>
<p>Once the BTC Seller receives your payment they will confirm in Bisq and your security deposit and your purchased trade amount will be available in your wallet.</p>
<span class="wiki-legacy-anchor" id="How_do_I_sell_BTC_with_my_Paxum_account?" aria-hidden="true"></span><h2 id="how-do-i-sell-btc-with-my-paxum-account"> How do I sell BTC with my Paxum account? </h2>
<p>You can sell BTC with Paxum on Bisq in 2 waysː</p>
<ol>
<li>Make an offer to sell BTC on the XXXXXX market.</li>
<li>Take an offer to sell BTC on the XXXXXX market.</li>
</ol>
<p>When the offer is confirmed Paxum will provide with the BTC Buyer with your Paxum details for you to complete the payment. They should send payment at their first opportunity and confirm they have done so in Bisq by clicking 'payment started'.</p>
<p>Once you receive payment from the BTC Buyer you should confirm payment has been received in Bisq. One you have done this the BTC Buyer will receive their funds and you will receive your security deposit back.</p>

<p>If a dispute occurs the mediator may ask the buyer to send them information showing that they made the payment of the correct amount to the BTC Seller within the correct time frame.</p>
<p>The mediator may ask the seller to send them information to prove their account details are correct, or in the case of the BTC Buyer making a payment late, or of the wrong amount.</p>
