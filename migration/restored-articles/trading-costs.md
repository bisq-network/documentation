<div class="wiki-recovered-article" lang="en"><p>Traders incur <strong>trading costs</strong> to do trades using the Bisq trading protocol.</p>&#10;<span class="wiki-legacy-anchor" id="Trading_fees" aria-hidden="true"></span><h2 id="trading-fees"> Trading fees </h2>&#10;<p><a href="../trading-fees/">Trading fees</a> are paid to the Bisq DAO to fund development of the Bisq software.</p>&#10;<p>These fees are payable in BTC or BSQ, with BSQ fees targeted at offering a discount of 25% over BTC fees.</p>&#10;<span class="wiki-legacy-anchor" id="Mining_fees" aria-hidden="true"></span><h2 id="mining-fees"> Mining fees </h2>&#10;<p>Bitcoin <a href="../mining-fees/">mining fees</a> must be paid to do on-chain bitcoin transactions, and each Bisq trade currently requires 4 on-chain transactions: maker fee tx, taker fee tx, deposit tx, and payout tx.</p>&#10;<p>Offer makers only pay for the first one (maker fee tx) while offer takers pay for the other three (taker fee tx, deposit tx, and payout tx).</p>&#10;<span class="wiki-legacy-anchor" id="Premiums" aria-hidden="true"></span><h2 id="premiums"> Premiums </h2>&#10;<p>Another potential "cost" is the difference between <a href="../bisq-price-indices/">spot price</a> offer price (also known as spread). Spread is set by offer-makers and tends to go down as liquidity improves.</p>&#10;</div>
