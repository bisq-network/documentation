<div class="wiki-recovered-article" lang="en"><p>First download, install Bisq and create a backup (copy seed words - see <a href="../how-to-back-up-your-bisq-wallet-seed/">How to back up your Bisq wallet seed</a>).</p>&#10;<p>Make sure you have a <a href="../fiat/">fiat</a> payment account set up. For example see <a href="../set-up-a-sepa-payment-account/">Set up a SEPA payment account</a> or <a href="../set-up-a-revolut-payment-account/">Set up a Revolut payment account</a>.</p>&#10;<p>1. On <strong>Market</strong> tab select EUR - Euro</p>&#10;<p>2. Click on <strong>BUY BTC</strong>.</p>&#10;<p>3. Select an offer and click on <strong>TAKE OFFER</strong>.</p>&#10;<p>4. Review offer details including amount, price. Then click on <strong>NEXT STEP</strong>.</p>&#10;<p>5. Fund your trade (from Bisq wallet or external wallet).</p>&#10;<p>6. Click on <strong>CONFIRM TAKE OFFER</strong>.</p>&#10;<p>7. After 1 blockchain confirmation (~10 minutes) you will be prompted to initiate payment. Make a transfer to the seller using your fiat payment method, then click on <strong>PAYMENT STARTED</strong>.</p>&#10;<p>8. When the seller confirms payment received the trade is complete and you can <strong>withdraw your BTC</strong>.</p>&#10;<ul>&#10;<li>For more short guides see <a href="../howto/">Howto</a>. You can also find support on <strong><a href="https://bisq.chat" rel="noopener noreferrer">Matrix</a></strong>.</li>&#10;</ul>&#10;</div>
