## Roles

Bisq records contributor roles, ownership and periodic reports in the [roles repository](https://github.com/bisq-network/roles). A role description can define duties, the access needed to perform them, owners, reporting and a bond. The historical framework distinguished maintainers, operators and administrators: maintainers guide an area and its process, operators run infrastructure, and administrators maintain an account or service. Actual authority comes from the current role issue and project permissions, not this summary.

New roles are proposed through the [proposals repository](https://github.com/bisq-network/proposals); the roles repository asks contributors not to open a role issue for an unapproved role. Coordinate with its maintainer when an approved role is assumed. The older process also described adding a secondary owner and transferring ownership, with a documented handover and reports. Check the current issue before assuming the same workflow or a role is vacant.

## Bisq Maintainer

The recovered description assigns software maintainers pull-request integration and critical-bug coordination, in addition to common reporting duties. It historically listed repository administration and a required bond. See [role issue 63](https://github.com/bisq-network/roles/issues/63); current access and bond conditions need confirmation.

## Community Manager

The historical role followed public conversations and surfaced product updates, user feedback, support patterns, misinformation and opportunities to the relevant team; it also described coordinating campaign messages. See [role issue 95](https://github.com/bisq-network/roles/issues/95). The recovered Growth Team page is marked retired, so do not infer that its channels or meeting schedule are current.

## Compensation Maintainer

The role description covers communicating cycle information, triaging compensation issues, connecting submitted DAO transactions to requests, and recording accepted or rejected outcomes. The historical board states and deadlines are described in [compensation and reimbursement](../../dao/requests/); check [role issue 86](https://github.com/bisq-network/roles/issues/86) for current practice.

## Mempool Explorer Operator

The recovered role ran a Bitcoin/Bisq-enabled explorer, made it available through HTTPS and Tor, and coordinated its inclusion in relevant client/provider configuration. It listed HSTS and DNSSEC, a 20,000 BSQ bond and up to $150 per month in the historical description; these amounts and operational requirements are not verified current. See [role issue 107](https://github.com/bisq-network/roles/issues/107) and verify requirements with maintainers.

## Monero Explorer Operator

The recovered role operated a Monero explorer with HTTPS and Tor access, maintained its infrastructure, and coordinated client configuration. The historical description listed a 20,000 BSQ bond and $100 per month; these are not verified current entitlements. See [role issue 109](https://github.com/bisq-network/roles/issues/109).

## Payment Method Maintainer

The recovered role vets proposed payment methods, maintains method requirements and a roadmap, and monitors problems and market coverage. This is a process and risk-assessment role, not necessarily software maintenance. See [role issue 38](https://github.com/bisq-network/roles/issues/38); the former monthly-reporting and KPI descriptions need current confirmation.

## Pricenode Operator

A pricenode operator maintains an available, up-to-date price service and its environment. The older description included Tor, monitoring and provider-list coordination. Running a personal node does not automatically make it a default provider for other clients. See [role issue 14](https://github.com/bisq-network/roles/issues/14) and [infrastructure guidance](../infrastructure/). The historical description listed $130 per cycle and a bond; those figures are not a current promise.

## Proposals Maintainer

The recovered description covers triaging proposal issues, maintaining process documentation and reporting on the role issue. It historically listed write access to the proposals repository. See [role issue 30](https://github.com/bisq-network/roles/issues/30) and [proposals and voting](../../dao/governance/).

## Roles Maintainer

The recovered description covers documenting role changes and coordinating assignment, unassignment and reporting. See [role issue 28](https://github.com/bisq-network/roles/issues/28). Consult the issue for present ownership and permissions.

## Security Team Lead

The recovered page explicitly marks this role retired. It recorded a security roadmap, a point of contact for reports and review of team compensation; it does not establish a current lead. Use the relevant repository's security policy for reporting vulnerabilities and see [role issue 108](https://github.com/bisq-network/roles/issues/108).

## Support Agent

The recovered role describes monitoring support channels, helping users, reporting product issues and submitting cycle reports. Its old named-agent list, hours, rates, internship and retention rules are not a current roster or policy. Verify an agent's identity through current official channels, and share the minimum necessary information privately. See [support work and transaction context](../support/) and [role issue 64](https://github.com/bisq-network/roles/issues/64).

## Support Team Lead

The historical role coordinated support schedules, calls, internships, compensation review and repository maintenance. See [role issue 102](https://github.com/bisq-network/roles/issues/102); do not infer current staffing or a current internship program from the snapshot.

## Wiki Operator

The recovered MediaWiki operator description covered DNS and hosting, plugin maintenance, database and filesystem backups, off-site copies and issue triage. Its named infrastructure and access rights are historical, not instructions to modify the present documentation deployment. See [role issue 92](https://github.com/bisq-network/roles/issues/92).

## YouTube Admin

The recovered description covered uploading videos, responding to comments, and maintaining featured playlists. It recorded account-management access and bonding; check [role issue 56](https://github.com/bisq-network/roles/issues/56) for current ownership and requirements.
